# India job-source comparison — 4 October 2026

This is a small, role-specific pilot for one experienced R&D, hardware and quality leader in Chennai/Bengaluru. It is not a market-wide estimate. The app shows a job only after it verifies the employer vacancy's title, location, description, date and company application page.

Its medical IoT, electrical safety and hardware leadership duties make it a strong candidate for full profile matching. Parallel Wireless was mostly specialised telecom testing. |

The employer feeds are free and particularly strong for authenticity and direct applications, but each feed covers just one company. A curated set of promising employers is better suited to this client than attempting to ingest every technology board. Current live integration starts with Dozee and Instawork. The Greenhouse verifier now uses its official Job Board API for pages that omit JobPosting structured data, while retaining strict employer, title, location, deadline and description checks.

Pilot Naukri searches (25 records maximum each): hardware engineering head/Bangalore, medical device R&D/Bangalore, head of quality/Chennai, automation consultant/Chennai. All four completed on the free credit. The published actor pricing gives an estimated $0.043 for 100 records and four starts; billing in Apify Console is authoritative. No $10 top-up is needed for this evaluation.

The hardware query returned 25 jobs, largely software engineering leadership rather than hardware engineering. The medical-device query returned 25 jobs, mostly unrelated software roles; ABB's product-development head and Hitachi Energy's electrical R&D engineer merit individual review, although the latter asks only 4–5 years of experience. The Chennai quality query returned 25 jobs, with a handful of potentially suitable manufacturing or supplier-quality leadership roles. The automation-consultant query returned 25 jobs, almost entirely software test automation roles. Raw keyword matches overstate suitability: 23 quality titles and 25 automation titles were detected, yet many refer to software testing, junior posts, or unrelated employer names. Ranking must use the complete description and seniority.

An employer-page spot check found [Hitachi Energy's Supplier Quality Manager in Chennai](https://www.hitachienergy.com/careers/open-jobs/details/JID3-216438) published 16 September 2026, with a real `Apply now` button and a job function of Quality Management. [Pfizer's Manager, Quality – Digital Systems](https://www.pfizer.com/about/careers/job/4963070) also has an official Chennai posting, but its Veeva eQMS/GxP specialism requires further profile evidence before recommending it. These demonstrate that a Naukri lead can sometimes be resolved to a direct company page, but that process needs employer-level verification per listing. The actor itself does not solve the required direct-application-link problem.
