# Medical search pipeline (20 October 2026)

The search form starts with **Focus on medical fields** enabled. A candidate can turn it off; that choice persists. The company directory contains 168 distinct official medical device, diagnostics, life science, pharmaceutical and related equipment career entry points, including employers in Chennai, Bengaluru, Madurai and other parts of South India. A directory entry is a discovery lead, not evidence that the company has a suitable opening.

When the focus is enabled, a search attempts every directory career page with at most six concurrent page reads. It records page successes and failures separately, and saves up to 50 plausible vacancy links per employer in the private source inventory. The discovered link titles are compared with every role entered on the form. Previously unchecked links are tried first, with stronger role titles ahead of weaker titles. Up to 36 individual vacancy pages are read per search; the saved unreviewed links move forward on subsequent searches even if the index page later becomes unreadable. A rotating set of four companies whose pages had no readable vacancy links also receives a domain-restricted web lookup. This helps with sites that load jobs in JavaScript or block direct reads. Each vacancy still needs a current posting date, employer ownership, job title, description and eligible location before it can be recommended. Failed pages and lookup calls are logged and cannot produce invented jobs.

After directory discovery, the app groups every entered role into direct employer web searches before rotating through employer feeds, saved sites, Naukri, JSearch and description matching. Role coverage can be cut short by the per-run time or web lookup limits, and JSearch cannot run after its provider quota is exhausted. The search stops when the requested number of new recommendations is found, the per-run budgets are reached, or no more usable leads are found. Past recommendations are excluded from new result lists and remain visible in Job tracker. Daily email alerts remain paused until the owner resumes them.

The directory lives in `app/medical_directory.py`. Add only employer-owned career URLs, with a distinct company name and field. The detailed verification rules are in `app/direct_links.py`, and the directory scanner is in `app/medical_crawl.py`.
