# Expanded employer discovery

The search keeps the candidate's editable roles, skills, work types and recent-posting choice. Its default places now include South India, resolved into individual cities for source queries and checked against the actual vacancy location. Medical and life-science roles remain the first preference; suitable textile and other engineering roles remain eligible.

Each run checks the public Lever and Greenhouse employer feeds, a bounded official Baxter career index, a rotating sample of senior roles from Danaher's public job sitemap, saved career-site hints and verified company sites, targeted employer-site web search, two small Naukri searches, and up to 36 JSearch requests when its provider quota permits. Radiometer's official Danaher career site is now in the seed list. The sitemap scanner remembers which links it checked and rotates through the rest on later runs. Every returned company can enrich the persistent company inventory. Unverified site hints are used only to discover possible openings; they do not become trusted application links. Individual vacancies must still pass employer title, hiring company, location, description, availability and posting-date checks before ranking. A Danaher subsidiary vacancy is accepted only when the official vacancy text names that subsidiary.

The search caps are 90 employer vacancy checks, 16 employer web lookups and 15 minutes per run. The requested number of jobs remains the output cap. JSearch capacity is checked before each run; an exhausted JSearch plan does not prevent employer-site or Naukri searches from finishing. The app's atomic $30 monthly paid-API stop and owner notification remain in force. The admin API use page reports recorded provider spending; JSearch estimates are not an invoice and can include calls covered by the free plan.

The official career-site seed list is in `app/company_memory.py`. These are discovery starting points, not verified job claims. They rotate across runs, and the list of companies from searches grows independently of the seed list.

Hitachi Energy's structured vacancy summary is short. For that employer, verification now requires the full job-description block from its public vacancy page and passes those duties to the matching model. Previously cached checks are refreshed under verification version 4.
